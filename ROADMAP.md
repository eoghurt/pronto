# Roadmap

Ideas not in v0.1, roughly in order of usefulness.

- **Editable marks after reopening.** Saving flattens marks into the page. Saving
  them as real PDF annotations (Highlight, FreeText, Ink) as well would let you
  move or delete them in a later session, and in other apps.
- **Fillable form fields.** PDFs with real form fields could show them as boxes
  you tab between, rather than typing on top.
- **Find (⌘F)** using the text layer that's already there for highlighting.
- **The 8 icon colourways** from the lookbook's "Pick your colour" page, switchable
  in the app (needs a native call to change the Dock icon).
- **Typed text in Figtree.** Typing uses Helvetica so the saved file matches the
  screen exactly; embedding Figtree needs its TTF and fontkit.
- **Combine PDFs** by dropping a second file onto the Pages rail.
- **Print (⌘P)** and a real macOS share sheet on the share button.
- **Signed and notarised builds** (needs the paid Apple Developer Program) so the app opens without a Gatekeeper warning, and Finder's "Always Open With" works on downloaded PDFs.
